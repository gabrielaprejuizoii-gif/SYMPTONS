Grammar focus: how to use “pain”

A) be in pain

= suffering, in general (no location).

  • I’m in pain.
  • She’s in a lot of pain.
  • He looked like he was in pain.

B) have pain

= pain + where / what kind / when. Uncountable.

  • I have abdominal pain. ✅
  • I have an abdominal pain. ❌ unnatural
  • I have pain in my lower back.
  • I have a sharp pain in my chest. (a + adjective: ok)
  • I have pain when I walk.

C) have a/an + -ache

Countable, specific. Common: headache, stomachache, toothache, backache, earache.

  • I have a headache.
  • I have a toothache.

Pain × ache × sore

  • Pain: general word, can be mild or severe → sharp, stabbing, burning, severe pain.
  • Ache: dull, continuous discomfort → a dull ache, body aches.
  • Sore: tender/painful to touch or use → sore throat, sore muscles, my legs are sore.
  • “a pain” (specific): a sharp pain in my side. Also informal: “He’s such a pain.” = annoying!

Location · intensity · duration

Where: in my chest / lower back / right side / behind my eyes.
How strong: mild · moderate · severe · unbearable · “on a scale of 1 to 10, it’s a 7”.
How long / pattern: since yesterday · for three days · constant · comes and goes · worse at night.
Type: sharp · dull · throbbing · burning · stabbing.

Situation A — At the pharmacy 💊

You’re traveling abroad and start feeling sick. You walk into a pharmacy.

Useful phrases

  • I’ve been feeling sick since yesterday. · I think I’m coming down with a cold.
  • I’ve been running a fever. · I have a persistent cough.
  • My throat is really sore. · I’ve been feeling dizzy.
  • Do you have anything for a headache? · Could you recommend something for this?
  • Do I need a prescription? · How often should I take it? · Are there any side effects? · Should I take it with food?

Dialogue

Pharmacist: Hi, how can I help you?
You: Hi. I think I’m coming down with a cold. My throat is really sore and I have a persistent cough.
P: How long have you been feeling this way?
You: Since yesterday. I’ve also been running a fever.
P: Are you allergic to any medication?
You: No, I’m not. Could you recommend something for this?
P: This should help with the sore throat. Take one every six hours.
You: Should I take it with food? Are there any side effects?
P: It may make you a bit drowsy, so be careful if you’re driving.
You: Got it, thank you. If I don’t feel better, I’ll see a doctor.

🎭 Roleplay (teacher = pharmacist)

Start with “Hi, how can I help?” and add surprises, one at a time:

  • “Sorry, we’re out of that brand. Is a generic OK?”
  • “Are you taking any other medication?”
  • “Is it a dry cough or a wet cough?”
  • “Could you repeat that? I didn’t catch it.” (student must rephrase)
  • “Do you have a fever right now?”
  • “That one needs a prescription. Do you have one?”
Professora: anote erros para feedback no final. Se ela travar, ofereça o começo da frase (“I’ve been…”). Reforce o gesto de pedir repetição: “Sorry, could you say that again?”

Situation B — At the doctor’s office 🏥

The symptoms are getting worse, so you make an appointment.

Say it like this

  • I’ve been feeling unwell for three days.
  • The pain started last night.
  • It hurts when I breathe deeply.
  • The pain comes and goes.
  • I’ve been experiencing nausea.
  • I haven’t been sleeping well.
  • I feel exhausted all the time.
  • The symptoms have gotten worse.
  • I’m allergic to…
  • I’m currently taking medication for…

Doctor’s questions → your answers

  • What’s bothering you? → I’ve had a bad cough and chest pain.
  • How long have you been feeling this way? → For about three days.
  • Where exactly does it hurt? → Here, on the right side.
  • Constant, or does it come and go? → It comes and goes.
  • Have you taken anything for it? → Just paracetamol.
  • Allergic to any medication? → Yes, I’m allergic to penicillin. / No.
  • Any other symptoms? → Yes, I have chills and body aches.

Dialogue

Doctor: Good morning. What’s bothering you today?
Patient: I’ve been feeling unwell for three days. I have a dry cough and it hurts when I breathe deeply.
D: Where exactly does it hurt?
P: Here, in my chest. The pain comes and goes.
D: Have you taken anything for it?
P: Just some pain relievers. The symptoms have gotten worse, and I haven’t been sleeping well.
D: Any other symptoms?
P: I’ve been running a fever and I feel exhausted all the time.
D: Are you allergic to any medication?
P: No. And I’m not taking any medication right now.
D: Let me examine you. Please take a deep breath.
P: Sorry, could you explain that again? I want to be sure I understood.

Professora: roleplay: você é a médica. Pergunte na ordem acima e depois improvise: “On a scale of 1 to 10…?”, “Does anything make it better or worse?”, “Have you traveled recently?”. Ao final, a aluna confirma o que entendeu: “So, I should rest, drink water and come back if it gets worse?”

Activity 1 — Look and Describe

Activity 2 — What would you say?

  1. You have a terrible headache and need something for the pain.
  2. You’ve been coughing for three days and your throat hurts.
  3. You ate something at a restaurant and now you feel nauseous.
  4. You’re traveling alone and suddenly feel dizzy.
  5. You have a rash on your arms and don’t know what caused it.
Possible answers (after she tries)
  • I have a terrible headache. Do you have anything for the pain?
  • I’ve had a cough for three days and my throat is really sore.
  • I think it was something I ate. I feel nauseous. Could you help me?
  • Excuse me, I suddenly feel dizzy. Could I sit down, please? I’m traveling alone.
  • I have a rash on my arms and it’s itchy. I don’t know what caused it.

Writing practice ✍️

You’re abroad and feel sick. Write a short message to the hotel receptionist (5–6 sentences). Include: what you feel · when it started · your symptoms · any medication taken · what help you need.

Model (open only after you write)

Hello, this is Ana from room 204. I’m not feeling well. I started feeling sick last night, and I’ve had a fever, a sore throat and a headache since this morning. I took some paracetamol, but it hasn’t helped much. Could you recommend a doctor or a clinic nearby? I’d also appreciate it if you could help me make an appointment for today. Thank you so much for your help.

Professora: compare o texto dela com o modelo; destaque o que ela fez bem e 2–3 melhorias (polidez: Could you… / I’d appreciate it if…).

Homework — “The Last Time I Got Sick”

Write 120–160 words. Answer:

  1. When did it happen?
  2. Where were you?
  3. What symptoms did you experience?
  4. How did you feel physically?
  5. How long did the symptoms last?
  6. Did you go to the doctor or the pharmacy?
  7. What medication or treatment did you use?
  8. Did it help?
  9. How did you feel when you recovered?

Use: I was feeling… · I had… · I was experiencing… · I started feeling… · I went to… · I took… · The symptoms lasted… · Eventually, I recovered.

Professora: peça que ela leia em voz alta na próxima aula e conte a história sem olhar o texto.
